## A path through the sounds

This editorial selection uses existing preset recipe names. Inclusion in the distributed bank and audio examples await release confirmation. No demo audio is advertised as available in this draft.

Start with **INIT**, then **SINO DE OBRA** (struck iron and FM), **CHAPA ARRASTADA** (scraped steel), **TACA CANTANTE** (singing glass), **LATA CAINDO** (bouncing metal), **MOTOR VELHO** (irregular motion and distortion), **VENTO NO CABO** (a steel cable with granular surroundings), and **VA GORDO** (detuned wave stacks). Listen to the attack, sustain and release; try gentle and strong notes, then macros if assigned by the preset.

Presets by Ruído. Names keep their library spelling. To explore SAMPLE, capture an object of your own as described in the manual; no factory recording is assumed.
